FAQ

Does Caliccio collect any data?
No. Caliccio has no network access, no analytics, and no accounts. See the Privacy Policy.
Why does my answer show as a fraction/root instead of a decimal?
Caliccio defaults to exact results where possible. Switch to Decimal mode in Settings if you'd prefer a rounded number.
Is Caliccio available on iPad?
Caliccio is currently iPhone only.
