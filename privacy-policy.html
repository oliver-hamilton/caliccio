Privacy Policy for Caliccio

Effective date: 18 August 2026

Caliccio ("the app") is developed by Oliver Hamilton ("we", "us").

Data collection

Caliccio does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or usage data of any kind. Specifically:

  • The app makes no network connections. All calculations happen entirely on your device.
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services are used.
  • No accounts, sign-in, or user profiles exist.
  • Nothing you type into the calculator is saved, logged, or sent anywhere — it exists only in memory while the app is open and is discarded when the app closes.
  • The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or any other device permission.

Third-party services

Caliccio uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's privacy

Caliccio does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised effective date.
